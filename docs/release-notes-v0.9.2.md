## What's New in Version 0.9.2

Two additions to the Fantasy Cheatsheet.

### Rookie Filters

The **Position** filter now includes **Rookies**, along with **Rookie QB**, **Rookie RB**, **Rookie WR** and **Rookie TE**, so you can look at just the first-year players — or just the first-year players at one position — without leaving the board.

ESPN's fantasy data carries no rookie flag, so Scores works it out: a player with no statistics from any earlier season. That catches undrafted rookies as well as drafted ones. It would also flag a veteran who sat out a whole season, so treat it as a strong hint rather than a guarantee.

The rookie status of any player is shown on their details screen, alongside their ranks and projections.

### Export to CSV

**Export to CSV** saves the draft board to a spreadsheet file. It always writes **every** player, not just the ones your filters are currently showing — filtering is something a spreadsheet does well on its own.

Each row carries the PPR and Standard ranks, player, position, team, rookie flag, injury status, average draft position, auction value, projected points in all three scoring formats, and whether you have marked that player drafted. Opening the file in Excel keeps the numbers as numbers and the accented names intact.

### Upgrading from 0.9.1

Scores will offer the update itself, or run `Scores-0.9.2-Setup.exe`.
