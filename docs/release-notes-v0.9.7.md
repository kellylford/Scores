## What's New in Version 0.9.7

### Football games are back

In the middle of the NFL and college football seasons, every week said "No games found for this week," the current one included, and stepping to other weeks didn't help.

The cause was a change on ESPN's side. Scores asked ESPN for each football week as a range of dates, Thursday to Monday for the NFL. ESPN stopped accepting date ranges, for every sport, and now answers one with an error, so every week came back empty. Football weeks are now requested by week number and season instead, which ESPN still supports. Regular-season, preseason and postseason weeks and the college bowl slate all load again.

### Team schedules and venues

The same change emptied a few other screens that asked for a range of dates:

- **Team schedules** for the WNBA, college basketball and the soccer leagues
- **The venue list**

They now ask ESPN for one month at a time and show what they did before. Schedules for the NFL, NBA, NHL, MLB, college football and college hockey use a different ESPN feed and weren't affected.

### Upgrading from 0.9.6

Scores will offer the update itself, or run `Scores-0.9.7-Setup.exe`.
