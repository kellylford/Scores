Example of Enhanced Basketball Play-by-Play (iOS-Style Format)

BEFORE (Generic Format):
```
Period 2
  John Doe makes three point shot
  Jane Smith misses layup  
  Team timeout
  Bob Johnson makes free throw
```

AFTER (Enhanced iOS-Style Format):
```
Period 2
  [12:34] John Doe makes three point shot (45-42)
  [11:58] Jane Smith misses layup (45-40)
  [11:58] Team timeout (45-40)  
  [11:45] Bob Johnson makes free throw (46-40)
```

Visual Indicators Applied:
- 🟦 Light blue background: Made 3-pointers
- 🟩 Light green background: Made layups/dunks/tips  
- 🟨 Light yellow background: Made free throws
- 🟥 Light red background: Missed 3-pointers
- 🟧 Light orange background: Regular fouls
- 🟠 Darker orange background: Technical/flagrant fouls
- ⬜ Light gray background: Timeouts
- 🟪 Light purple background: Substitutions
- 🔵 Light blue-green background: All scoring plays

Benefits:
1. Shows action, score, and time together as requested (iOS-style)
2. Color coding helps users quickly identify play types
3. Chronological order (most recent plays first) improves readability
4. Maintains all existing accessibility features
5. Works for NBA, NCAA Men's Basketball (NCAAM), and NCAA Women's Basketball (NCAAWB)
